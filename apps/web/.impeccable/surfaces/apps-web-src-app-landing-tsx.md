# Landing (cloud mode, ozvi.io /)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: indie founder or small studio choosing a feedback tool (compares with Canny, Featurebase, Fider). Must believe: it looks like their app, bugs come with context, the loop closes with the reporter, it is cheap and can run on their own server. Proof: the real product UI (route, themed board, pricing from code). No invented customers, numbers or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show a report travelling its route. Refuses the SaaS template (hero + four icon cards + three pricing cards).

OWN-WORLD: Dark, technically confident product page: "Ozvena" mark (o + two echo arcs), indigo on night ink, Geist display and text, Geist Mono for code and measurements; hero shows the host app and the live event log in sync (anime.js); spec sheet of real technical facts; tabbed integration code; numbers as tracking numbers.

STORY: A bug gets a number, moves stop by stop, the reporter confirms the fix. Then: the same board wears your brand; two spaces (private bugs, public ideas); two lines to embed; a tariff table.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Yellow band on top, header (Ozvi, links, Prihlásiť sa, Začať zadarmo). Left: "Každé hlásenie má svoju trasu." at ~64px, one paragraph, two actions. Right: a large shipping-label card of bug #128 whose route fills stop by stop once on load and ends at "Opravené vo v2.15, funguje vám to?".

FORM: Sledovanie zásielky (surface in the established world), seed f6136868.

SIGNATURE: the route filling once on load; the tariff table as a courier price list.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
