# Board (self-host and cloud): ideas, bugs, roadmap, novinky, detail, forms, admin

Mode: Operate (Novinky reads as Read). Audience: host-app users (infrequent, non-technical, arrive signed in from the app) and the host team (frequent, triage between other work). Board adopts the host brand through --oz-* tokens; the world lends scale, density, shape language, state vocabulary and one signature move. Owner rejected the previous UI as weak and generic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every report is a shipment with a number and a route. The board is a tracking overview where you see where your thing is right now and what comes next, not a forum of floating cards. Refuses the category default: a narrow centered column of rounded white cards with vote boxes and pill tabs.

OWN-WORLD: Shipping label and tracking page. Board: host surface as label stock, host ink as label print, host accent as the route line and actions; states in a fixed shape vocabulary (hollow, dotted, half, filled-check, double, slashed) that reads in any host palette; numbers as tracking numbers in tabular heading type; rows separated by rules inside one panel, not stacked cards. Ozvi surfaces: Ozvi preset (indigo on night ink, Geist).

STORY: User sees their report's stop and the next stop, votes or adds detail, and later gets "opravené vo v2.15, funguje vám to?". Team reads the queue by stage and moves things along in one place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width top bar (brand left, tabs Nápady/Roadmapa/Novinky/Hlásenia chýb, account right) over a 1180px frame. Ideas: title + count + search and stage filter on one line; main list (vote, number, title, one-line excerpt, meta, mini route with current stop named) left; right rail with "Naposledy doručené" (recently shipped) and the route legend; primary "Navrhnúť nápad" top right. Detail: content + comments left, right rail with the Trasa timeline (dated stops, team replies, GitHub, release) and team controls.

FORM: Sledovanie zásielky, rank 1 on my list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key f6136868.

SIGNATURE: the Trasa route: mini stop-rail in every row, full dated vertical timeline on the detail page, lanes on the roadmap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
