# 0006 — Pricing: Creem, quarterly/yearly, limits on costs

**Status:** accepted 2026-11-05

**Decision.** Free €0, Pro €19/year (€6/quarter), Studio €49/year (€15/quarter). Payments through Creem (as MoR it handles VAT). No monthly payments — the fixed $0.40 fee would eat 20 % of €2. We limit only attachments and e-mails; not users or entries. Details and cost estimates: the pricing on ozvi.io.

**Risk.** A price that is too low may look like a toy → in Pro, visibly show SSO, GitHub and MCP.
